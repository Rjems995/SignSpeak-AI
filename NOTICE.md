# Third-party material

The expanded movement coordinates in `datasets/motion-expanded.json` and the installed J/Z model combine SigNN's CC0 source described below with **Garg, Bindu; Kasar, Manisha; Kashyap, Achyut; Vats, Amber; Sharma, Gunjan; Hange, Aditya (2025), SignAlphaSet, Mendeley Data, V3, doi:10.17632/8fmvr9m98w.3**, https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/8fmvr9m98w/3 , under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: selection of original AVI recordings, MediaPipe landmark extraction, rejection of unusable videos, temporal normalization, deduplication, preservation of source labels/provenance, grouping non-J/Z controls as unsupported, training and browser-weight export. No endorsement is implied. Archive/file hashes and attributions are retained in the motion provenance manifests. The static SignAlphaSet review remains excluded from static training; original AVIs form a separately audited motion source.

A small independent **ASLLVD research benchmark** was evaluated locally: **Neidle, Carol; Thangali, Ashwin; Sclaroff, Stan (2012), Challenges in Development of the American Sign Language Lexicon Video Dataset (ASLLVD) Corpus, LREC 2012**. [Original source, full credits and terms](https://www.bu.edu/asllrp/av/dai-asllvd.html). Its terms permit research/education and prohibit redistribution and commercial use without permission. It does not contribute training, validation, support points or reference animations. Restricted clips, source annotation subset, signer mapping and coordinates remain under ignored local storage; only aggregate results are retained. `MOTION_DATA.md` documents the frontal-view selection, capture-time correction and annotation-based trimming. No ASLLVD redistribution license is implied by the rest of this project.

The additional training coordinates in `datasets/asl_hg-landmarks.json` and `datasets/public-balanced.json` derive from **Pranto, Md. Famidul Islam; Islam, Md. Rifatul; Akbor, Md. Ali; Ghosh, Nabonita; Alam, Md. Rahatun; Chaki, Sudipto; Islam, Md Masudul (2025), ASL-HG, Mendeley Data, V1, doi:10.17632/j4y5w2c8w9.1**, https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/j4y5w2c8w9/1 . License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: deterministic selection from the original raw archive, MediaPipe coordinate extraction, validation, normalization, deduplication and signer/letter balancing. Original P1–P10 signer identifiers are namespaced. Physical hand, per-image lighting, angle and session remain unknown. No creator endorsement is implied. Attribution, source archive hashes, and metadata limits are in `datasets/public-sources.json`.

Two further static-image sources were inspected locally and **excluded from static training**. **Garg, Bindu; Kasar, Manisha; Kashyap, Achyut; Vats, Amber; Sharma, Gunjan; Hange, Aditya (2025), SignAlphaSet, Mendeley Data, V3, doi:10.17632/8fmvr9m98w.3**, https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/8fmvr9m98w/3 , is CC BY 4.0. Its unidentified signer cohort, unannotated handedness and unclear per-file image augmentation status prevent its use as evidence of new physical left hands or signer-independent performance. Its license is the dataset's license; the related paper has separate publication terms. Original AVI motion recordings are handled by the separate import documented above.

**Barczak, A. L. C.; Reyes, N. H.; Abastillas, M.; Piccio, A.; Susnjak, T. (2011), A new 2D static hand gesture colour image dataset for ASL gestures, Research Letters in the Information and Mathematical Sciences, 15, 12–20** describes Massey's MU HandImages ASL dataset. The [original public page](https://www.massey.ac.nz/~albarcza/gesture_dataset2012.html) requests citation and carries Massey University copyright (2012); no explicit redistribution license was located. Local inspection used a pinned JPEG mirror (`snrao310/ASL-Finger-Spelling-Recognition`, revision `5d1f82ac72676f8901ff1aeca8d85a5a06f0d6ae`). Neither its images nor extracted coordinates are bundled or used in training. The saved import audit records filenames, counts and the source's lighting-direction convention, without assigning CC BY or another invented license. Raw archives and review coordinates remain in ignored local caches.

The new static coordinate data in `datasets/aslyset-landmarks.json` and the balanced subset derive from **Rivera, Miguel (2019), ASLYset, Mendeley Data, V1, doi:10.17632/xs6mvhx6rh.1**, https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/xs6mvhx6rh/1 . License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: extraction of MediaPipe hand coordinates, rejection of invalid detections, coordinate normalization, deduplication, metadata curation, and selection of an equal-count subset. No endorsement by the dataset creator is implied. Source archive hash and attribution are in `datasets/aslyset-provenance.json`.

The bundled public landmark data in `datasets/signn.json`, the prepared coordinate dataset, and model artifacts derived from that data originate from [AriAlavi/SigNN](https://github.com/AriAlavi/SigNN), revision `40bd651899e09a0099767457194062f77f774156`.

SigNN's upstream repository license is Apache License 2.0. A copy is preserved at `datasets/SIGNN-LICENSE.txt`. Provenance and limitations are recorded in `datasets/provenance.json`. Modifications here include deduplication, coordinate normalization, grouping unsupported labels, training a new classifier, and exporting browser inference weights and evaluation artifacts.

Upstream contributors listed in the SigNN README: Arian Alavi, Vahe Barseghyan, Rafael Trinidad, Gokul Deep, Kenny Yip, John Kirchner, Daniel Lohn, and Conor O'Brien.

MediaPipe and TensorFlow.js are loaded from their public distributions. Their licenses remain with their distributions. ASL University / Lifeprint learning references are linked to their original website. Interface icons and hand illustration are original SVG source within this project.

The added J/Z motion sequences and learned movement model derive from the SigNN Team public video dataset, version 1, published on Kaggle under CC0: Public Domain. Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/signnteam/asl-sign-language-alphabet-videos-j-z . `datasets/motion-provenance.json` records exact file names and hashes. Raw clips are ignored by Git; retained coordinate sequences are extracted by MediaPipe and used to train a new temporal classifier.
